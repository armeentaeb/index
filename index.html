I am an assistant professor in the Department of Statistics at the University of Washington. I was a postdoctoral fellow at ETH Zürich, mentored by Peter Bühlmann. I obtained my PhD in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Caltech, mentored by Venkat Chandrasekaran.

How do we make the process of scientific discovery reliable and robust? My research is driven by this question, from controlling false discoveries in phylogenetic trees and other complex structures, to making causal inferences under uncertainty, and to synthesizing from multiple and heterogeneous data sources.
